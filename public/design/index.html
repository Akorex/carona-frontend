Carona design

Everything that used to live on claude.ai: the 21 laptop screens from the Carona Screens canvas and the Carona design system. The screens are the canvas files themselves, so links between them work like the canvas's Play mode.

Play the screensStart at Welcome and click through. The bar at the bottom moves to the previous or next screen (← →), shows the canvas note and switches light/dark. Design systemPrinciples, colour, type, layout, every component with its props, and the Building with Carona guide.

The notes come from the canvas as it was on 20 Nov 2026. Some are out of date: PATCH /users/me and PATCH /vehicles/{id} now exist, and the backend has POST /rides/ (searching rides and bookings are still to come).
